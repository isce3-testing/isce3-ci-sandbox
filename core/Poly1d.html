Poly1d¶

Poly1d is a data structure meant to capture 1D functions of the form

\[f(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{N} c_k \cdot \left( \frac{x-\mu}{\sigma} \right)^k\]

where

  • \(N\) is the order of the polynomial.

  • \(\mu\) is the mean.

  • \(\sigma\) is the norm.

  • \([c_0, c_1, ..., c_N]\) is the set of coefficients.
